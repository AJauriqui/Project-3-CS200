# Project-3-CS200

## 1. Simulation Overview/Example
Our project simulates an operating hospital using patients, doctors, and B-Trees. This simulation tracks patients entering the hospital, waiting for treatment, doctor assignments, and eventually being discharged. Time advances one hour at a time, and allows the user to see how the hospital changes throught the simulation. To begin, 8 patients are admitted into the waiting room. Each patient has a name, date of birth, age, condition level, arrival time, and critical waiting time. The patients are organized in a B-Tree based on their condition level, arrival time, and birthday. Patients with worse conditions have higher priority to be treated. If two patients have the same condition level, the patient who was there first has priority. Doctors also have a B-Tree that stores their information including their name, birthday, skill level, and assigned shift. Each doctor works and 8-hour shift, and their skill level determines how much they can improve a patients condtion per hour of treatment. Doctors can only accept new patients during their shift times, but they can continue treating a patient after their shift ends. Each time the user advances the simulation by one hour, doctors treat their assigned patients, and patients whose condtion reaches zero are discharged. Available doctors on the current shift are then assigned patients from the waiting room based on priority. Patients who remian in the waiting room become one condition worse per hour. If a patient waits 5 hours without receiving treatment, they are marked as critical. Finally, two new patients are admitted into the hospital
Example: If 2 patients are waiting for treatment, Patient A has a condition level of 8, and B has a condition level of 5. Patient A receives priority because they have a more severe condition. If an available doctor has a skill level of 3, Patient A's condition decreases from 8 to 5 after one hour of treatment. The doctor continues treating the patient until their condition gets to 0, which then means the patient gets discharged.
For our visual component, our project uses SFML to display a visual representation of the hospital. It includes a waiting room showing patients and their condition levels, along with four doctor offices showing each doctor's name, skill level, shift and current patient. Patients are represented by colored circles based on their condition or critical status. The visualization also includes animations that represent patients moving from waiting room to the doctor's office. Users can advance the simulation one hour at a time by pressing the spacebar or clicking the advance button. The can also press R to automatically advance the simulation about one time per second. The dashboard displays the current simulation day and hour, allowing users to see how patient conditions, doctor availability, and the waiting room change over time.
